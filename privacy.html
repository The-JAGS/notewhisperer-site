Privacy Policy

Note Whisperer · Last updated 11 September 2026

Note Whisperer ("the app") is designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your data. Note Whisperer does not operate any servers, does not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

  • Audio recordings and transcripts are stored locally on your device only.
  • Your OpenAI API key is stored in the device's secure Keychain and never leaves your device except to authenticate directly with OpenAI.

Third-party processing (OpenAI)

To transcribe and analyze your recordings, the app sends your audio and the resulting text directly from your device to the OpenAI API, authenticated with your own API key. This transmission happens between your device and OpenAI; it does not pass through any server operated by us.

Your use of the OpenAI API is governed by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy. Per OpenAI's API data usage policies, data submitted through the API is not used to train their models by default. Please review:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API Data Usage Policies

Microphone

The app uses the microphone solely to record the voice notes you choose to create. Audio is only captured while you are recording.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, as it collects nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
